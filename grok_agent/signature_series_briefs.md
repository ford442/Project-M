## orbital_rave_zephyr

- **Lane:** Zephyr Orbital · `orbital_rave_*`
- **Music:** uptempo electronic, drum & bass, festival drops
- **Look:** cyan/magenta neon tunnel; kaleidoscope segments; bass pumps tunnel depth
- **Audio:** `bass_att` → tunnel scale + hue; `treb_att` → strobe on composite highlights
- **Avoid:** heavy CPU per_pixel; muddy brown palette
